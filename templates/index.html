      Awareness & Official Reporting

      Understanding Cyber Crime & How to Report It

      Cyber crime covers any illegal activity carried out using computers, networks, or digital devices—ranging from phishing and financial scams to identity theft and online harassment.

      Official Government of India Portal · MHA / I4C

      National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

      Managed by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs, cybercrime.gov.in enables citizens across India to report online financial fraud, phishing, social media abuse, and other cyber offenses, upload evidence, and track complaint status.

      Visit cybercrime.gov.in ↗
      Financial Fraud Helpline Dial 1930

      If You Are Targeted or Scammed

      1. Do not share OTPs or PINs: Disconnect immediately and change passwords on affected accounts.
      2. Preserve evidence: Save the suspicious URL, screenshots, sender IDs, and transaction references.
      3. Call 1930 immediately: For UPI, card, or bank fraud, rapid reporting helps freeze fraudulent transfers.
      4. File online: Submit your complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and keep the Acknowledgement Number.

      Phishing & Spoofed Sites

      Fake emails, SMS alerts, or cloned websites imitating banks, courier services, or login pages to steal credentials and OTPs.

      UPI & Financial Fraud

      Fraudulent collect requests, deceptive QR codes, screen-sharing scams, fake loan apps, and unauthorized card transactions.

      Identity Theft & Takeover

      Misuse of personal documents, SIM-swap attacks, and compromised email or social media accounts used to defraud contacts.

      Malware & Ransomware

      Malicious attachments, trojans, or pirated software that lock files for ransom, log keystrokes, or siphon saved passwords.

      Online Harassment

      Cyberstalking, fake impersonation profiles, blackmail/extortion, and non-consensual distribution of private content.

      Tech Support & Job Scams

      Fake customer care numbers in search results, scareware pop-ups, or fraudulent job offers requesting upfront verification fees.
